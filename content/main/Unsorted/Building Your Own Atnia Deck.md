The first set of Atnia cards, The New World, contains ~550 unique cards for you to play with. You can find the full list of cards on this [Cubecobra Page](https://cubecobra.com/cube/list/Atnia?view=spoiler). 

Rules for constructing your deck:
- Your deck must contain at least 60 cards.
- Your deck may contain no more than 3 cards with the same name, except for Core locations, which you may include any number of (there is one available for each ideal).

Tips for navigating the Cubecobra page:
- You can switch to a visual grid view by clicking on the square icons above the full list of cards.
- You can use the 'filter' field to help you find cards for your deck.
	- Enter in a card's name to find it.
	- Use `type:creature` to find units, `type:artifact` to find items, `type:land` to find locations, and `type:instant` to find spells.
	- Use `tag:Arca` to find Arca cards. You can do the same for the other ideals and `multi` ideal cards.
	- Use `notes:cardtext` to search the contents of all cards' text boxes. You can use this to find all cards with a given ability such as `imbue`, all cards that `draw` a card, and much more!

Tips for starting out with deck construction:
- For single ideal decks, you will want to play zero core locations and explore for all of your locations, and/or play non-Core locations.
- For decks with two ideals, you can use 20-25% of your deck being Core locations (or others which add devotion to two or more ideals) as a starting point. However, an optimal density has not been determined and your devotion requirements impact how many you want to play. You may want to play a few more locations in decks with 3+ ideals.

## Printing Your Deck
If you would like to print your deck in paper, the easiest way to do this currently is to write write your decklist as a text file / discord message and send it to @skifreak. From there I will be able to send you a pdf containing only the cards you need for your deck. It is technically possible for you to do this step yourself, however I would likely have to spend more time writing instructions and helping with troubleshooting than it would take for me to simply do it myself and send you a printable file.
