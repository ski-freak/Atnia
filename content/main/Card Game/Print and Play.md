Print and play materials can be found in this [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1uaPVVRm0TW8_3MQ1KiiAEczAYbrZNUPy). To start out, print out the following:
- The helper cards
	- The cheat sheet cards are useful as references while playing, and the others can be used along with dice to track your resources while playing.
- Within the prebuilt decks, print both of the learning decks and the 'extras for starters' cards.
	- Shuffle each deck separately and give one to each player. Keep the extra cards on the side while you play and do not shuffle them in.

Also have on hand (either through printing or your phone) the 'Parts of a Card' graphic and Keyword Dictionary (most cards in the learning decks have reminder text for their keywords, but a few don't).

Before/while playing, you can familiarize yourself with the rules [[Card Game Rules Doc]]. We do not currently have proper quick start rules, but hopefully the rules page will work well enough for the time being.

For a short introduction to the setting the card game takes place in, check out [[Atnia Introduction]].

If you would like to build your own decks from the 550 available Atnia cards, see [[Building Your Own Atnia Deck]]
