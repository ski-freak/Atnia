View all cards from the set [here](https://cubecobra.com/cube/list/Atnia?view=spoiler).

This document will give a few suggested strategies you can build your deck around for each of the seven ideals. This list is not exhaustive, there are many smaller themes consisting of just a few cards as well as possible strategies players will inevitably come up with themselves. The goal here is to give a quick overview and starting point for deckbuilding and drafting.

Something kind of notable for those coming from MTG draft is that due to to there being 7 ideals, most themes are centered on a single ideal rather than two or more. You will almost always include multiple ideals in your deck, so have fun combining cards and strategies!

# Arca
##### Chaos, The Rift, Arcane Magic, Change
Arca represents a chaotic and wild energy that has expanded throughout Atnia after the apocalypse. Highly magical, both destructive and creative, to many it is symbolic of the new world.

### Interaction

Damage cards as they are being played (or your opponent directly), and force your opponent to discard their cards.

# Talentus
##### Martial Arts, Armies, Skill & Craftsmanship
Talentus represents mundane skills as well as combat prowess. Low on magic / magic takes a more subtle form. Magic tends to be either magical weapons, or being supernaturally skilled at something.

### Interaction
Use your units to disrupt your opponent's strategy.

### Swarm / Multiple Attacks
The Burning Plain contains many dangers; warring factions, undead soldiers, and hordes of demons. One of the two major aggro decks, Talentus is able to overwhelm opponents with huge waves of small units. Many cards benefit from you attacking multiple times in a round, and having multiple units also allows you to do that!

### Cache

The Fifth Legion manages the distribution and storage of supplies for much of eastern Atnia. This is integral for keeping the army fed and local villages safe from the horrors of the burning plain. Cached are stored for later, able to be played the round you cache them and the next, and afterwards are discarded.

# Epoca
##### History, Ancient Secrets, Past Civilizations
Drawing from ancient magics & past civilizations. While also very magical, it is in many ways the opposite of Arca, as it represents a romanticization of the past and opposition to the chaotic new world. Strongly connected to the coins/currency of the old Atnian kingdom.

### Interaction

### Clerics / Destroy Allied Cards

After the apocalypse, Atnia's connection to the gods has been severed. In this new world, many clerics have gone mad and some even resort to sacrificing their fellow people to perform divine magic. This strategy combines cards that do things when they die with cards that kill off your own cards for some benefit. Beyond this there are a few notable cards such as Pale Sun's Influence which you can utilize particularly well here (you can attack, play Pale Sun's Influence, then attack again this time with the units created from it killing your cards).
