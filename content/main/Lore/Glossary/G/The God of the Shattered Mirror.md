Some claim to have seen him in their dreams -- A [[The Gods|god]] who matches much of the description of the god [[Hwain]], yet he has two good eyes instead of one. Those he speaks to, he often comes to often, contradicting himself or even declaring previous visitors to be impostors. He promises much to those he seeks out, but demands much in return. Some who speak with him say that if you look past the words of one of these incarnations and listen to the contradictory cacophony between them, an ancient, more primal intelligence's whisper can be heard on the edge of one's mind.
